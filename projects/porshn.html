What is porshn?

porshn is a simple app that splits bills and expenses across a group of users. By centralising users into private groups, everyone can be notified about new or incoming bills, assign paid or upcoming expenses more fairly, and coordinate chores alongside financial responsibilities.

The larger ambition is to make shared living smoother. In the same place where people split rent, groceries, and utilities, they can also manage who is handling what next.

Problem

No more "who owes who?" headaches

Instead of scattered reminders across messages, the product aims to centralise shared costs in one calm workflow.

Audience

Built from student life

The original use case comes from the reality of uni housing, shared budgets, and busy schedules.

Direction

Money plus household coordination

The concept expands beyond splitting payments by exploring chores, delegation, and shared accountability.

Why it matters

As a university student, I know how quickly a simple bill can become an awkward social loop. Everyone is busy, money lands at different times, and no one wants to keep chasing friends for their share. porshn is meant to reduce that tension and replace it with clarity.

The value is not only financial tracking. It is social relief: fewer follow-ups, clearer expectations, and a shared system that makes group living feel less chaotic.

Current product thinking

01. Shared groups

Private groups keep expenses in context so users only see the bills and tasks relevant to their household or team.

02. Incoming and paid bills

Track expenses whether they are upcoming, already covered, or waiting for others to settle their share.

03. Chore rotation

The concept explores letting groups assign chores and potentially use chore completion as part of contribution balancing.
